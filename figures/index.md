## Figure 16: How routing decisions can become routing capital

- File: [figure-16.svg](figure-16.svg)
- SHA-256: `e7daea14b5119cd95d02306426d9722aa471dca04de1a4b9978543820d017a51`
- Description: A conceptual two-timescale model. Within one engineering decision, the engineering question and current design state lead to a routing choice, evaluator evidence, and a decision to act, escalate, or stop. Across decisions, observed outcomes and human overrides are preserved with provenance and pass through a validity gate. Supported and relevant experience updates validated organizational capability and guides the next route. Tool, PDK, design, or method drift triggers retesting, requalification, or retirement. Experience that fails or bypasses the gate can become accumulated error and misguide future routes.
- Published caption: Figure 16. Routing capital connects two timescales. Within a decision, an engineering system routes the next evaluation and decides whether to act, escalate, or stop. Across decisions, observed outcomes become routing capital when provenance and validity checks preserve what remains reliable. Drift triggers retesting, requalification, or retirement. Experience that bypasses the validity gate accumulates error and can misguide later routes.
